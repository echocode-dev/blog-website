| Ingredient | Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| Coffee Beans | 20g |
| Water | 300ml |
| Temp | 94°C |

## Instructions
* Grind beans to a *medium-fine* consistency.
* Bloom the grounds:
    * Pour 50ml of water.
    * Wait 30 seconds.
